Dinosaurs are divided into major groups based primarily on their hip structure

Major Dinosaur Types

  • Theropods: Two-legged carnivores like Tyrannosaurus rex and Velociraptor.
  • Sauropods: Long-necked, massive herbivores like Brachiosaurus and Diplodocus.
  • Ceratopsians: Horned, frilled plant-eaters like Triceratops and Protoceratops.
  • Thyreophorans: Armoured or spiked dinosaurs like Stegosaurus and Ankylosaurus.
  • Ornithopods: Beaked, duck-billed herbivores like Iguanodon and Parasaurolophus.
