Dinosaur

Dinosaurs were a diverse group of reptiles that dominated terrestrial life on Earth for over 140 million years during the Mesozoic Era. They first appeared during the Triassic period (around 245 million years ago) and largely went extinct at the end of the Cretaceous period (about 66 million years ago). However, modern birds are directly descended from avian dinosaurs, meaning dinosaurs are technically still alive today.

Dinosaurs thrived across multiple geological periods in vastly different climates, adapting to changing landscapes that featured dense tropical forests, massive river systems, and active volcanic ranges.
