Football

Football is a popular team sport played with an inflated ball, best known globally as soccer (governed internationally by FIFA) and in North America as American football.

Main Types of Football

  • Association Football (Soccer): Two teams of 11 players try to kick a round ball into the other team's goal. Hands are mostly not allowed, except for the goalkeeper.
  • American Football: Two teams of 11 players try to score points by running or throwing an oval ball into the other team's end zone or kicking it through goalposts. Players use their hands and carry the ball.

Rules

  1. Objective: Score more goals than the opposing team by getting the ball completely over the goal line into the net.
  2. Team Size: Each team has 11 players on the field at a time, including one designated goalkeeper. A match cannot start or continue if a team drops below 7 players.
  3. Match Duration: Standard matches last 90 minutes, split into two 45-minute halves with a maximum 15-minute half-time break. Referees add stoppage time at the end of each half for delays.
  4. The No-Hands Rule: Field players cannot touch the ball with their hands or arms. Only the goalkeeper may use hands, and strictly within their designated penalty area.
