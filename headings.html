Tigers

The tiger is the largest wild cat in the world, famous for its reddish-orange fur with black stripes and its role as a powerful hunter. It lives in parts of Asia and is an endangered species.

Appearance

Tigers have unique stripe patterns like human fingerprints. They can weigh up to 660 pounds.

Habits

They live mostly alone, hunt at night, and love water unlike most other cats.

Diet

As meat-eaters, they hunt deer and wild pigs
