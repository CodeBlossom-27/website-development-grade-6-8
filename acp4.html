A mango is a sweet tropical stone fruit from the evergreen tree Mangifera indica, native to South Asia and cultivated for over 4,000 years. Often called the "king of fruits", it belongs to the cashew family and features a single central seed, juicy yellow-orange flesh, and smooth skin that ranges in color.
