About Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is an iconic ivory-white marble mausoleum located on the southern bank of the Yamuna River in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. Commissioned in 1631 by the 5 th Mughal Emperor, Shah Jahan, it houses the tomb of his favourite wife, Mumtaz Mahal, as well as the emperor himself. Globally recognized as a universal symbol of love and a masterpiece of Mughal architecture, this UNESCO World Heritage Site
